Seasonal Rodent Control Tips for Amarillo Residents (Winter vs Summer Infestations)

Published 2026-11-18 · Updated 2026-11-18 · By Amarillo Rodent Control

Rodent prevention is easier when it follows the calendar. This guide breaks the year into four short checklists, based on Extension guidance and what we see on Amarillo properties.

Spring: repair and trim

  • Check soffits, vent covers, and door sweeps for wind damage.
  • Trim tree limbs that come within 6 feet of the roof, and thin dense ivy on fences or walls.
  • Re-check crawl space vents and foundation gaps.

Source: UC IPM, Pest Notes: Rats

Summer: remove what draws them in

  • Use trash cans with tight lids and keep them clean of food residue.
  • Store pet food in rodent-proof containers and feed pets only what they finish.
  • Pick up fallen fruit and clear garden debris.
  • Keep compost in rigid containers with tight lids.

Source: UC IPM, Pest Notes: Rats (reproduction rates)

Fall: finish sealing

Cooler weather is when mice and rats look for shelter, so late summer is the time to close gaps larger than 1/4 inch with metal or concrete. Store firewood and boxes off the ground and away from walls.

Source: UC IPM, House Mouse (reproduction rates)

Winter: watch and listen

Listen for scratching in the attic or walls at night, and check garages and storage rooms for droppings. Mice can breed year-round indoors, so winter activity should not be ignored.

Source: NC State Extension, Biology and Behavior of Common Structure-Invading Mice (how mice multiply)

Where each Amarillo neighborhood may need extra attention

Neighborhoods on the city's edge, such as Greenways, Soncy, and Lawrence Park, sit close to open land, so a September check makes sense. Older, tree-lined areas such as Wolflin and Bivins may need a roofline check after winter wind. Areas near drainage, including Pleasant Valley and Llano, deserve a look at the yard and fence lines in spring.
