Rat Nest Removal in Amarillo, TX

Finding a rat nest means two things: an active colony has been established in your property long enough to build shelter, and the health and structural risks have escalated significantly. Rat nests are built from shredded insulation, paper, fabric, and any soft material the rodents can access. In Amarillo, they're most commonly found in attic insulation near soffit and ridge areas where radiant heat accumulates in winter, in crawl spaces near plumbing that provides warmth and water, and in wall voids near kitchen areas with food access.

Pups (juvenile rats) in a nest indicate an established breeding colony, which needs more intensive treatment than a transient population. Rats can breed year-round indoors, so a colony found in October may have been there for weeks and will keep growing without prompt treatment.

Health Risk of Rat Nests

Rat nests concentrate the pathogens associated with rodent infestation: dried urine, droppings, dead pups, and parasites such as fleas, mites, and ticks. Disturbing a nest without respiratory protection can put these into the air, which is why proper protective equipment matters for anyone doing nest removal.

Our Rat Nest Removal Process

Locate All Nests: Rodents often keep more than one nesting site. We use UV lighting and a systematic inspection to find all of them before removing any, because removing one while leaving the others can simply push the colony elsewhere.

Safety Protocol: N95 respirators, Tyvek coveralls, and gloves for all nest work. No exceptions regardless of nest size or apparent contamination level.

Removal and Disposal: Nest material bagged in double-sealed heavy-gauge plastic and disposed of properly. No compression or compaction during bagging.

Surface Sanitation: The nest area treated with EPA-registered disinfectant and allowed to dry before any additional work or re-access.

Damage Assessment: After nest removal, we check the surrounding area for gnaw damage to wiring, piping, and structural members and note it for your records.
