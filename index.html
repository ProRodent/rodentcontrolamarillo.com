Who We Are

Amarillo's Dedicated Rodent Control Team

The Texas Panhandle brings its own rodent challenges. Open farmland, strong seasonal winds, and big temperature swings can push rats and mice toward Amarillo homes. Amarillo Rodent Control was founded in 2023 to address exactly this: a rodent control company that knows local conditions and Amarillo neighborhoods and focuses on lasting fixes instead of temporary ones.

From Bishop Hills to Wolflin, from Downtown to Pleasant Valley, our technicians cover every ZIP code in Amarillo. We find the root cause, eliminate the infestation, seal every entry point, and follow up with monitoring, which goes well beyond setting traps. Prevention starts with finding and sealing the gaps rodents use.
